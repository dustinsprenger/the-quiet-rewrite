# The Quiet Rewrite

Cloud terms, government access and consent — a public evidence repository by Dustin Sprenger × ABEL (AI-assisted research).

[Read the website](https://dustinsprenger.github.io/the-quiet-rewrite/) · [Original PDF](dossier.pdf) · [Evidence ZIP](evidence.zip) · [Source library](https://dustinsprenger.github.io/the-quiet-rewrite/sources.html)

The June–October 2026 comparison covers Apple, Dropbox, Evernote, Microsoft, Runbox/Boxy and mailbox.org. The preserved 11-page PDF is unchanged. The October 28 supplement adds redacted Dropbox correspondence and a direct comparison finding identical wording in the two preserved government-disclosure sections.

Policy permissions, documented changes and actual disclosures are different propositions. These records do not establish covert government access or coordinated targeting. The evidence retains negative findings and uncertainties.

## Files and integrity

- dossier.pdf: byte-identical original dossier.
- report.html / dossier-text.txt: searchable transcription.
- evidence.zip: relevant public source snapshots, comparisons, receipts, verification records and redacted correspondence.
- evidence-manifest.json: original versus public hashes and redaction labels.
- SHA256SUMS.txt: current published-download checksums; evidence.zip contains a separate manifest.
- source-index.json: 35 indexed source records and evidence types.
- dropbox-government-comparison.json: exact compared passages, normalization method and hashes.

Private response cookies, local paths, email transport headers, tracking HTML, ticket links and routing identifiers are removed from public derivatives. Unrelated private account exports are excluded. Originals remain local and unchanged.

The site is static HTML/CSS, with no build dependencies and no added analytics. Search-engine requests do not guarantee indexing. Third-party source rights remain with their authors; no blanket licence is granted over them.
