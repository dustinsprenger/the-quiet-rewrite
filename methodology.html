Method and limits

Preserve the differences.
Preserve the doubts.

Three separate questions

  1. What did the policy say at each documented point?
  2. What changed between those versions?
  3. What evidence, if any, shows the policy being applied to an account?

The first two can be investigated through preserved texts and archives. A disclosure clause alone cannot answer the third.

Dates have different meanings

Posting dates, effective dates, archive captures, repository commits and retrieval times are separately identified in the dossier. A Last-Modified header is not treated as proof of a first-change date. The September 2 OSF publication date is an anchor, not evidence that a provider acted in response.

A reproducible record

The PDF is byte-identical to the retained original. The public evidence manifest maps original and published file hashes. SHA-256 values support comparison of bytes; they do not by themselves authenticate authorship, establish a date, or prove that a claim is true.

Privacy and public derivatives

Public retrieval receipts have response cookies and local filesystem paths removed where present. Correspondence is published as redacted plain text, with transport headers, tracking HTML, private support-ticket links and routing codes omitted. The original emails remain local and unchanged. An unrelated Mistral account export is excluded.

The evidence bundle includes a Runbox acknowledgment. Receipt of an inquiry does not establish that its concerns were investigated or accepted.

What is not established

These records do not establish coordinated action by the providers, covert government access to Dustin’s files, or a causal connection between the publication of his research and the changes described. Negative findings—including unchanged clauses and strengthened protections—remain part of the record.

Corrections and rights

Readers may raise a specific, sourced correction through the repository’s issue tracker. Please do not post private account records or credentials. Third-party policies remain their authors’ material; this project makes no blanket licence grant over them.

Research context

These works explain the author’s interests; they are not evidence of provider misconduct.
